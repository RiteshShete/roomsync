## Phase 8 decisions and deviations
- **Leave household** (You → Household settings → Leave household): step 1 explains the consequences (access until the date, history stays, open chores are passed on, **RoomSync does not move money, work out deposits or mark anything paid**, cancel any time before the date). Step 2 shows what is outstanding, read-only: your balance **labelled an estimate** (disputed or reversed payments aren't counted), open chores that will be passed on, recorded payments **in dispute** that involve you, and grocery items you added. Then the leaving date (today to +90 days; defaults to a week ahead so nobody leaves today by accident), an optional note, and an explicit tick before "Leave household". The balance you reviewed is stored with the move-out as `move_out_acknowledged_balance_minor`. A failed start keeps everything typed.
- **While leaving:** a status screen and a banner on every page. The person keeps full access until the date; the screen shows what was recorded, what is unresolved, and lets them add notes or disputes (listed, never resolved automatically) or cancel (with a confirmation).
- **Owner and sole member:** the owner is told to transfer ownership first (link to Members); a sole owner is pointed to archiving. The database enforces both (`owner_must_transfer`, `sole_member`).
- **Owner/admin starting it for someone else:** Members → Manage → Start move-out (date and note), or Cancel their move-out. Admins may now open Manage for non-owners, but role changes and ownership transfer stay owner-only (shown disabled with the reason). The owner can never be moved out by someone else.
- **After the date:** `reconcile_household` runs when a household is opened (best effort, silent on failure), so a person whose date has arrived is out the next time they open the app, and others stop seeing them as "moving out". They get a one-time **"You've left <household>"** screen (name and date only, via `my_left_households()`, which exposes nothing else), then the usual create-or-join screen; they have no access to the old household's data. The others keep the history, the member can no longer be picked for new expenses or chores, and their open chores were already passed on or flagged.
- **Storage kept small (owner request: no caching, very little database memory):** there are no cache or derived tables (balances are computed when asked). The only self-growing tables are now bounded: `activity_events` (and its read marks) older than 90 days and `invite_attempts` older than a day are pruned, 500 rows at a time, inside the daily reminder sync (no scheduler). Expenses, settlements and `audit_events` are never pruned. In the browser, unused query data is dropped after a minute (`gcTime`), and lists are always limited or paged.
- **Cleanup:** `daysBetween` moved to `domain/dates`; the "Back" label on the leave steps changed so it can't clash with the header's Back button.
- **Tests:** new pure date and form rules (+tests), 53 component tests for the leave flow (owner, sole owner, review contents including estimates and disputes, date boundaries, explicit confirmation, failed save keeps input, double click, archived, status, notes, cancel, admin start/cancel, the left screen, blocked storage, reconcile), 808 pgTAP assertions (`my_left_households`, pruning), and Playwright journeys with real accounts: leave with a future date → still active → the date passes (set through the local service key, **in test setup only**) → the person is out, the chore moves to the next person, history stays; cancel; leave today; admin start/cancel; owner and sole-owner rules.

## Phase 7 decisions and deviations
- **Owner decisions applied first:** buttons now use dark ink on coral/amber (AA contrast, about 7:1) and a flat grey fill when disabled, so disabled and ready look clearly different (commit 35e9bc4). **In-app chore reminders are in** (owner request). **Chat, points and QR codes are out** (cost, and QR could imply payments). The owner's nine code-quality rules are saved to memory and applied (see "Code-quality pass").
- **Announcements** (House → Announcements): title and message, author and time, pinned first with a Pinned chip, an "edited" marker. The author, an owner or an admin can edit or delete (soft delete); only an owner or admin can pin (the Pin button is not shown to others; Edit and Delete are disabled with the reason). Every change is version-guarded, so a concurrent edit gives "Someone just changed this announcement. We refreshed the list." Posting notifies everyone else; pinning notifies everyone but the pinner; edits and deletes are silent. Text is rendered as plain text (tested with markup).
- **Activity centre** (bell → /activity): the updates addressed to you, newest first, unread marked in text as well as colour, filter chips by category, mark one read (by opening it) or all read, one deep link per event type (a test requires a sentence for every event type). **Deviation:** PLAN said "grouped by category"; I used filter chips over a chronological list, which keeps the order people expect. Home shows the latest five under "Recent activity" with a link to all; the old disabled "Add your first item" button is replaced by working quick-add links.
- **Notification preferences** (You → Notifications): per-category switches (a missing key means on, so new categories show by default) hide that category from your own list and badge. Quiet hours (device clock, overnight windows supported) hide only the unread number on the bell; the screen says plainly there is no push, email or text. Rules are unit-tested in `domain/notifications.ts`.
- **In-app reminders:** `sync_chore_reminders(household, today)` (security definer, members only) creates a `chore.reminder` event for an open chore's assignee on its due date and every 3rd day while it is still open, only if the household's reminders setting is on and it is not archived. A unique index makes it idempotent (many members opening the app produce one). It runs the first time a device opens the household each day, so a reminder shows up when someone next opens the app: there is no scheduler and no push. The caller's local date is passed and must be within one day of the server date.
- **You tab:** profile (name, avatar colour), personal summary (you owe, you are owed, open chores for you; no rankings), notifications, privacy/help/your data (what is stored and not done, **Download my data** via `export_my_household_data`, and an honest "self-service account deletion is not available yet"), plus the household pages and Sign out.

## Phase 6 decisions and deviations
- **Chores tab:** Today · Upcoming · Completed (counts in the tab labels). *Today* = due today **or overdue**; *Upcoming* = later or undated (`choreBucket` in `src/domain/chores.ts`). Overdue is neutral by spec §8.6: "This chore is still waiting to be completed." plus the original date, no alarm colour, no points, no ranking. Mark as done shows "Nice work — task completed.", names who is next for a recurring chore, explains anyone skipped by rotation (not active) or that the next one has no assignee (an owner/admin is notified), and offers Undo (reopen). Reopen is refused with a clear message if a later occurrence was already completed.
- **Create/edit chore:** pure form model (`features/chores/formModel.ts`, 14 tests). Repeating needs a due date; rotation needs ≥ 2 people and keeps the user's order (↑/↓ buttons, not drag, so it works by keyboard). Edit keeps Save disabled until something changes, sends the version loaded, and on conflict keeps the edits and offers "Load the latest version". Cancel chore asks first. Permissions in the UI mirror the database: assignee/creator/admin may complete or edit; anyone may complete an unassigned chore; archived disables everything, each with a stated reason.
- **Groceries (House → Grocery list):** progress bar, Needed/Purchased tabs, grouped by category. Ticking buys; unticking moves back. Add/edit sheet with a **duplicate check** (Update existing / Add anyway). Delete is soft with an Undo toast. Every change is guarded by the item's `version` (and expected status), so two people buying the same item at once gives one winner and the other sees "Someone else already updated this item. We refreshed the list."
- **House tab** is now a hub: Grocery list works; Announcements, Inventory, Calendar, Concerns and Emergency contacts are listed as disabled with a stated reason (rule 5), never dead links.
- **Activity events for direct-write tables** (decision from Phase 1): database triggers now emit them (`chore.created/assigned/edited/cancelled`, `grocery.added/purchased/moved_back/deleted/restored`). To avoid duplicates when an RPC (e.g. `complete_chore`) already emits its own event, `private.ctx()` and `reconcile_household` set a transaction-local flag `roomsync.in_rpc`, and the triggers stay silent while it is on. Metadata carries only title/due date or item name. Mutation-checked in pgTAP (750 assertions).
- **Bug found by e2e and fixed:** `toAppError` returned `unknown` for an error that had already been mapped, so conflicts thrown by the api wrappers showed no message. It is now idempotent (unit-tested).
- **Tests:** 527 unit/component tests (24 files), 750 pgTAP assertions, 14 Playwright tests (4 new): recurring chore with rotation across three real accounts (plain member blocked, next goes to the next person, undo, completed history); edit with an offline failed save keeping the typed value, then cancel; grocery add → duplicate → buy → move back → delete → undo; a concurrent-purchase conflict between two browsers. Mutation-checked (conflict mapping, the Today bucket).
- **Known gaps (unchanged, flagged):** white text on coral/amber buttons is below WCAG AA (a one-line token change, awaiting owner call). Mockup features that are out of spec scope (chat, points, QR, photos, reminders, priority/time) are NOT built.

Save is disabled until valid and says why; any failed save keeps every field. Edit mode keeps Save disabled until something changes, sends the version it loaded, and on a version conflict keeps the user's edits, offers "Load the latest version", and retries with the new version. People who have since left stay in an existing split (marked) but can't be added to new ones.

"Why do I owe this?" (or "…am I owed this?") fetches only the caller's own lines and checks they sum to the displayed net; if they don't (or a 2,000-row cap was hit) it says so instead of showing a wrong breakdown.
- **Record settlement:** pick a suggestion or choose people; amount, date (not future), note; a review step with the sentence "This records a payment made outside RoomSync. RoomSync does not move money."; a warning (not a block) when the amount is more than the payer currently owes; confirm; a receipt-like record. Plain members may only record payments they made or received (admins any pair); the UI says so. Reverse/dispute/resolve follow the PLAN §7 matrix; a dispute requires a reason; disputed settlements sit in their own section and are not counted.
- **New data access:** `api/expenses.ts`, `api/settlements.ts`, `listAllMembers` (including people who left, so names show on history). Categories are a fixed list stored as lowercase keys; unknown/legacy values display capitalised rather than being hidden.
- **Staleness:** money queries refetch whenever a screen opens (and on window focus), but there are no live updates yet. If you sit on the Expenses tab while someone else records a payment, you see it after navigating or refocusing. Realtime is Phase 7. The e2e helpers reload to simulate "opens the app again".
- **Tests:** 469 unit/component tests in 21 files (96 new for Phase 5: form model, tab, list/filters, form, details, balances, record settlement, settlements) and **10 Playwright tests** (5 new): three real accounts, ₹1,000 split → exact balances → "Why do I owe this?" adds up → settlement from a suggestion → edit after the settlement shows the impact → new balances; delete + restore and Undo; a dispute removes a settlement from balances; **permission denied in the UI and by calling the database directly** (`permission_denied`). ## Phase 2 decisions and deviations
- **`preview_invite` / `join_household` return `{ok:false, error:'invalid_invite:<reason>'}` instead of raising.** PLAN §6 says RPCs raise these. A raised error rolls back the failed-attempt row, so the 10-failures/hour throttle could never trigger. The `src/api` wrapper (Phase 3/4) must turn `ok:false` into the matching client error. Other errors (`already_member`, auth) still raise.
- **Argument style:** typed `p_*` parameters; shares are a JSON array `[{member_id, share_amount_minor, percent_bp?}]`. Return values: `create_invite` → `{invite_id, code, expires_at}` (code shown once), `edit_expense` → `{version, settlements_exist, impact[]}`, `complete_chore` → `{next_chore_id, next_assignee_member_id, skipped_member_ids}`, `reopen_chore` → `{cancelled_successor_id}`; mutating RPCs on versioned rows return the new `version`.
- **`private.ctx()`** is the single entry point for auth, reconcile, membership and archive checks. Non-members always get `not_found`.
- **Rotation only considers `active` members** (a `leaving` member is skipped; they can still receive a manually assigned chore or expense).
- **Settlements may involve `left` members** so their unresolved balance can be settled explicitly; balances of left members stay listed.
- **Settlement transitions:** recorded→reversed (admin/recorder), recorded→disputed (admin, or the other party who did not record it; reason required), disputed→reversed (admin/recorder), disputed→recorded (admin, or whoever raised the dispute). `reversed` is final.
- **Audit snapshots omit expense notes**; activity metadata has titles/amounts only.
- **Deferred constraints:** RPCs that depend on them call `set constraints all deferred` first and `immediate` after (found by tests: a mode change earlier in a transaction broke `transfer_ownership` and the second `add_expense`).
- **SQL mirrors:** `private.next_occurrence` and `private.next_assignee` mirror `src/domain/rotation.ts`; `private.compute_balances` mirrors `src/domain/balances.ts`. Shared vectors: `rotation.test.sql` and `balances.test.sql` vs `domain.test.ts`. Splits are validated (not computed) in SQL; the ₹1,000/3 vector is in `expenses.test.sql` and `constraints.test.sql`.
- **Test design:** each RPC test file starts from the same fixture (4-member House A created through the real RPCs, House B, an outsider) and logs in as each user via `set_config('role'/'request.jwt.claims')`. 700 assertions in 11 files. Mutation-checked (breaking the balance rule, the recurrence rule and the admin check each failed tests).
- **Generated types:** `npm run db:types` → `src/lib/database.types.ts` (unformatted; the CLI suggests `npx oxfmt`, not applied).
- **UI mockups received** (`UI/`, 29 PNGs): notes and conflicts with the spec are in `docs/ui-reference-notes.md`; decisions needed before Phase 3/4/6/7.

## Phase 1 decisions and deviations
- **`expense_shares.household_id` added** (not in PLAN §6) and `unique (id, household_id)` on `household_members`, `expenses`, `chores`. This allows composite foreign keys, so the database itself guarantees payer, participants, assignees, settlement parties and activity actors belong to the row's household. Phase 2 RPCs must fill `household_id` on shares. The deferred constraint trigger therefore only checks sums, equal spread (≤ 1 minor unit), percentage totals (10000 bp) and that each percentage share is within 1 minor unit of its exact value.
- **`created_by`, `recorded_by`, `completed_by`, `deleted_by`, `status_changed_by` are `auth.users` ids**; `*_member_id` columns are member ids.
- **Direct writes** (chores, grocery, announcements, notification prefs, profiles) are restricted by column-level grants + RLS `with check` + guard triggers. A pgTAP test pins the exact list of tables with direct INSERT/UPDATE; nothing has DELETE. Chores are only directly editable while `open`; complete/reopen is RPC-only. Cancelling needs admin or creator (enforced in the policy `with check`).
- **Activity/audit emission for the direct-write tables** (grocery, announcements, chore create/edit/cancel) is NOT built yet. PLAN §6 says triggers should emit them; PLAN §8 places activity emission in Phase 6/7. I will add those triggers in Phase 6 unless told otherwise.
- **Error codes:** invariant violations raise `23514` with message `validation:<field>:<code>`; vocabulary errors (`currency_locked`, `permission_denied`) raise `P0001`.
- **Owner rules:** unique partial index (one non-left owner) + deferred trigger (a household with non-left members must have an owner at commit). Ownership transfer must demote first, then promote, in one transaction.
